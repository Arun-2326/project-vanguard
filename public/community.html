FAQ

Good questions, honest answers.

Is this the official 3Blue1Brown website?

No. Epicycles is an independent fan project made out of appreciation. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by 3Blue1Brown or Grant Sanderson. For the real thing, visit 3blue1brown.com or the YouTube channel.

Will my message reach Grant?

Not directly — this mailbag is read by the fans who run this site. If you want to contact 3Blue1Brown, use the channels listed on the official website.

What do you do with my data?

Submissions and newsletter sign-ups are stored privately on the server so we can read and reply to them. Your email is never displayed or shared, and you can ask for your data to be deleted at any time by replying to any email from us.

Can I submit something I made?

Yes, please! Choose "My own math explainer" and include a link. Videos, articles, interactive pages and Manim experiments are all welcome.
